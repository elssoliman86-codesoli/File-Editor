# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-20

- Initial offline Arabic document workspace.
- TXT editing, legacy encodings, Arabic search and Regex replacement.
- Partial OOXML/DOCX editing with package-part preservation.
- PDFium viewer, text search/extraction and page organization.
- Images-to-PDF, Arabic PDF generation, native sharing and printing.
- Local recents/favorites, recovery snapshots, templates and themes.
- SAF export with read-back SHA-256 verification.
